# Multiverse of Monsoon

## Page 1 — Project and model design; drift, blur, and failure modes

**Mission.** An offline, CPU-only climate digital twin for India on the supplied 32 × 36, one-degree ERA5 grid. It accepts three physical-unit daily states and predicts a coupled, autoregressive rollout. A localized physical perturbation creates an alternate initial condition; the same model then evolves that branch.

**Deployed engine.** The submitted checkpoint is a gated local-transport model with a shallow residual convolutional backbone. It predicts climatology-anchored anomalies and advects them with bounded local wind transport. Land/terrain and local physical features condition the dynamics. No global pooling, flattened spatial fully connected layer, external weather feed, or future-case lookup is used at inference.

**Six dynamic fields.** T2M — 2 m temperature (K); TP — total precipitation (mm/day); U10 — eastward 10 m wind (m/s); V10 — northward 10 m wind (m/s); MSL — mean sea-level pressure (Pa); TCWV — total column water vapour (kg/m²). The service returns physical units and clamps precipitation to nonnegative values and temperature to the contract range.

**Stability and causal response.** Residual anomaly retention and seasonal climatology anchoring limit drift; multi-step training exposes the model to its own rollout errors. Rain-aware, spatial-variance, and gradient loss terms target precipitation misses and excessive smoothing. Bounded transport and local convolutions constrain early shock spread. Inference is deterministic; a zero perturbation follows the same path as an unperturbed request.

**Measured local evidence — not hidden-test results.** On 62 validation windows from 2020–21: valid physical outputs on 62/62; day-4 persistence wins on 60/62; mean RMSE skill 0.0955 (project target 0.12); heavy-rain CSI skill 0.0919 (target 0.10); anti-blur 10/10; complete shock checks in 2/5 stress regimes. The 2023–24 organizer windows were not available for this evaluation. These proxy numbers do not establish qualification or operational forecast skill.

**Failure modes.** A one-degree grid cannot resolve local convection or terrain-scale rainfall. Seasonal anchoring can suppress rare extremes; the local proxy indicates heavy-rain skill remains below target. Autoregressive uncertainty grows with lead time, and passing physical bounds does not prove forecast accuracy. This is a scenario and decision-support prototype, not operational NWP, a probability forecast, or an official warning system.

## Page 2 — Live visualization and monsoon impact use case

**Live multiverse dashboard.** Timeline Prime and each shocked branch are generated by the running `/simulate` service. The interface compares the same lead day and channel across Prime, Branch, and exact Branch-minus-Prime fields. The map uses bundled country geometry and local grid rendering; it has no hosted tiles or runtime network dependency. Users can inspect all six fields, pin a grid cell, and explore a 10–14 day dashboard horizon. The API accepts up to 30 steps for extended judge-led experiments.

**Shock Lab.** Judges can choose warm Bay of Bengal, Arabian Sea wind stall, null, or custom physical deltas. The branch is recomputed from the perturbed final input state, not altered after inference. Per-channel maximum absolute response makes coupled effects visible even when the selected map channel is not directly forced. A null shock is an invariance check, not a separate forecast.

**Impact extension — monsoon continuity and rainfall extremes.** Five live screens summarize forecast-period rainfall total, longest run below 2.5 mm/day, days at or above the model benchmark of 10 mm/day, maximum rolling three-day total, and cumulative departure from matching seasonal climatology. India summaries are weighted by cosine latitude and bundled land fraction; selecting a cell reveals its daily rainfall trace. These are model-derived exposure screens, not observations, IMD warnings, calibrated flood probabilities, or crop-loss estimates. Crop-water stress is not modeled because crop stage, evapotranspiration, and soil-water inputs are absent.

**How to interpret the branch.** A visible difference measures sensitivity to a changed initial condition under this model. It is not forecast uncertainty, observed truth, or evidence that the forced scenario is likely. The dashboard intentionally labels climatology initialization as a scenario and does not ship held-out validation cases or future observations.
